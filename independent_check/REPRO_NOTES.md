# Independent row-ledger reproduction

Run `python repro_ledger.py` in this directory. It reads only the two raw CSVs named in the task and the decisions encoded in the script. The outputs are `repro_row_ledger.csv` (one disposition for each stable `sheet_index:data_row_number`), `repro_stock_codes.json`, and `repro_reconciliation.json`.

## Judgment calls

- **Raw identity and ordering.** The duplicate key is the tuple of all eight CSV text fields, including case, spaces, blank strings, numeric spelling, and timestamp spelling. The first occurrence means sheet 1 in CSV order followed by sheet 2 in CSV order. No field is trimmed, parsed, or normalized before matching. Cross-sheet multiset matches take priority over within-remaining-row exact duplicates. Each sheet 1 occurrence can absorb one identical sheet 2 occurrence, even when the sheet 1 occurrence is itself a later exact duplicate.
- **Cross-sheet search window.** Because the date is part of the exact key, only sheet 1 rows whose timestamps fall within the intersection of the two sheets' raw timestamp ranges need to be held as potential matches. All sheet 2 rows are still checked. This changes memory use, not the matching rule.
- **Invoice syntax.** A numeric invoice is one or more ASCII digits over the entire raw field. `A` and `C` prefixes are case sensitive, and the rule for each applies before quantity or price tests. There were no invoices outside those forms after duplicate dispositions.
- **Exceptional merchandise codes.** `47503J ` (the raw trailing space), `PADS`, `SP1002`, and every individually listed `DCGS...` code are products. Their descriptions identify physical items such as garden tools, cushion pads, a chalkboard, gum, party bags, collars, and other accessories. For `DCGS...` codes with blank or `ebay` descriptions, I inferred merchandise from the same code family's described items; this is a judgment for those individual entries, which are all explicitly listed in the script and JSON.
- **Exceptional non-product codes.** `POST` and `DOT` are postage; `C2` is carriage; `M` and `m` are manual lines; `D` is discount; `S` is samples; `BANK CHARGES` is a bank charge; `AMAZONFEE` is a marketplace fee; `CRUK` is commission; `ADJUST` and `ADJUST2` are adjustment lines; `B` is bad debt; `TEST001` and `TEST002` are tests despite the description “test product”; all individually listed `gift_0001_...` codes and `GIFT` are vouchers. `C3` has only a blank-description, zero-value stock movement, so I conservatively classed it `unknown_non_product`. `GIFT` is also blank and zero-value; its code name is the basis for the voucher choice. No unlisted exception is silently classified.
- **Missing customer IDs.** Only the raw empty string is missing. Those rows remain in every money and order calculation; only distinct identified-customer counts exclude them. Customer identifiers are otherwise treated as raw strings.
- **Value and dates.** Line value is `Decimal(Quantity) * Decimal(Price)` without rounding, and cancellation value is the absolute value of each `cancel_product` line. GPS and CPV are dated by each line's own `InvoiceDate`, including any December 2010 rows in sheet 1. Year endpoints are inclusive calendar dates. NPR is GPS minus CPV. Distinct orders use raw numeric invoice strings with at least one retained `sale_product` line; distinct identified customers use raw nonempty customer strings with at least one such line. December 2011 is retained as a partial month and excluded from Year B.

## Data observations

- The sheets overlap from 2010-12-01 08:26:00 through 2010-12-09 20:01:00. The exact multiset match removes copied sheet 2 rows; later exact duplicates within the remaining stream are separately recorded and may include genuine repeated scans.
- Eighteen raw prices are `0.001`. Their line values mean some exact totals have three decimal places, despite the plan's “to the penny” phrasing. The two reconciliation identities are checked with full Decimal precision; rounding first would lose information.
- Negative-priced bad-debt adjustments and non-product fees make some disposition totals negative. Zero-price stock movements carry zero value but still count as rows. All raw rows were assigned and `unclassified` is zero.
